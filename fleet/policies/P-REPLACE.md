# Replacement vehicle authorization
Policy-ID: P-REPLACE
Section: 4.2
Version: 1.0

A replacement vehicle recommendation requires a vehicle in maintenance or unavailable status, an open high-severity maintenance event, and an active contract. An operator proposes the recommendation. A different authenticated approver must approve the exact evidence snapshot before a recommendation case is created. Approval expires after 15 minutes by default. The copilot never assigns a replacement vehicle or modifies a contract.
