# Repeated maintenance escalation
Policy-ID: P-MAINT
Section: 3.1
Version: 1.0

Escalate a vehicle when at least three maintenance events occur within the last 30 days. An unresolved critical telematics alert is also an anomaly. A support case may be proposed for any known vehicle. Operators must obtain approval from a different approver before creating any case or escalation. A case is a simulated internal record; it does not notify external people or systems.
