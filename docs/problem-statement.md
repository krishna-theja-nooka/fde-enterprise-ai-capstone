# Customer problem statement

**Fictional customer:** Northstar Fleet Services, a regional operator with two depots. **Users:** operations managers, service coordinators, and maintenance approvers.

The current workflow requires an operator to check a fleet spreadsheet, a maintenance log, contract dates, and policy PDFs before opening an internal case. Answers can disagree because the sources have different owners. An incorrect replacement decision or cleared safety hold could affect cost and vehicle availability.

Fleet Intelligence Copilot consolidates those read-only checks, shows source evidence, and prepares an internal action for human review. The system records who asked, which tools and policies were used, who approved, and which case was created. Every write remains visible and independently approved.

## Pilot acceptance

| Capability | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Explain vehicle unavailability | V-102's recorded safety hold and cited policy are returned |
| Find expiring contracts | Independent contract-ID expectations verify inclusive boundaries |
| Identify repeated maintenance | Counts distinguish three recent events from older events |
| Create an approved escalation | Execution is blocked before approval and succeeds afterward |
| Investigate a failure | Trace links request status to retries or missing evidence |

Operational targets are in the evaluation report. Real customer outcomes such as minutes saved, reduced downtime, adoption, and escalation precision are **not measured** by this synthetic pilot. A customer pilot would compare the same tasks before and after deployment, record operator time, and collect feedback from at least two roles before expanding scope.
